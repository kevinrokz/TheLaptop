💻 THE LAPTOP

Before You Start

You are investigating a mysterious laptop. Complete all 20 missions to finish the game.

  • 🎯 Missions must be completed in order, starting with Mission 1.
  • ⌨️ Use Arrow Keys to move around the 3D area.
  • 🖱️ Move the mouse to look around.
  • 🔵 Press E when the game says you can interact.
  • 🦘 Press Space to jump.
  • 💻 Press L or the Laptop button to open the laptop.
  • 🔋 The laptop battery can run out.
  • 🔌 If the battery is gone, you must search the house for the charger.
  • ⚙️ Settings is the official doorway to the house.
  • 🏠 You must use Settings → Enter House to enter the house.
  • 🛡️ The laptop contains MS Security and an antivirus scanner.
  • 🗃️ Threats can be moved to Quarantine.
  • 🤖 The laptop has an AI Helper that can give hints.
  • 🖥️ The laptop also contains MS OS.
  • 🏆 Mission 20 is the final investigation.

Important

Do not skip missions. Complete them in order. The game always begins at Mission 1.

⏸ PAUSED

Take a break. Your mission progress is safe.
