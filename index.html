📜 THE LAPTOP — 20 RULES

Read all 20 rules before starting the game.
1. You are the main player of THE LAPTOP.
2. Explore the laptop carefully and follow the current mission.
3. Use the desktop icons to open the laptop applications.
4. Use the Start button to find applications quickly.
5. Read mission instructions before doing the next task.
6. Keep an eye on the laptop battery.
7. When the battery gets low, look for the charger.
8. To go to the house go to settings.
9. In Settings, use the House option to enter the house.
10. In the house, inspect objects to find useful clues.
11. The charger is somewhere inside the house.
12. Return to the laptop after completing the house task.
13. Use MS Security / Antivirus when a threat is detected.
14. Quarantine suspicious files instead of ignoring them.
15. You may use the AI Helper when you need a clue.
16. Notes can be used to store clues you discover.
17. Files may contain secret information for missions.
18. Spreadsheet can be used for information and mission notes.
19. Minimize, maximize, restore, and close windows as needed.
20. The final mission requires you to open Terminal and TYPE ALL OVER by entering commands.
